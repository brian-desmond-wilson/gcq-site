How we play

Competitive, not cutthroat. We want to win, and we want everyone to want to come back. Captains set lineups so every player gets real innings.

Kickball, the grown-up version. Think baseball with a big red rubber ball: pitch it rolling, kick it, run the bases. If you can kick, you can play.

Who's here

Players make up each tournament roster. Entourage come along to cheer, wear the jersey and join every team dinner.

Everyone is welcome who wants a queer, joyful, safe team, whatever your gender, sexuality or skill.

Team values

Show up for each other

On the field, at the hotel and in the group chat.

Respect, always

Toward teammates, opponents and umps. No exceptions.

Privacy matters

Not everyone is out everywhere, so this site never lists our members.
