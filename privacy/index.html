Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy covers the Gym Class Queeroes iPhone apps (our "apps"), run by the Gym Class Queeroes kickball team ("we", "us"). That's the current Gym Class Queeroes app and the original app some members still use. The apps are a private space for team members, captains, substitutes and their invited guests. You can only join with an invitation from the team.

The short version: we collect what the team needs to play, travel and keep in touch. Your information is visible to your teammates, never sold, and never used for advertising or tracking you across other apps. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Information you give us

  • Contact info: your name, email address and, if you add it, your phone number. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Profile: anything you add, such as photos and cover photos, nickname and "alter ego" story, pronouns, birthday, home city, Venmo handle, social media handles, bio, fun facts, jersey number, positions, uniform size, drinking preference, favorite memories and music picks.
  • Emergency contact: the name and phone number you provide, so team leaders can reach someone if you're hurt.
  • Dietary and allergy info: dietary restrictions and allergies you add for yourself or your guests, so meals and events can be planned around them.
  • Your posts and uploads: posts, comments, reactions, polls and poll votes, chat messages, photos, videos (including their sound) and photo tags, and the GIFs you pick.
  • Team activity: event RSVPs, availability, award votes, game stats and lineups, checklists, reports you file about content or members, and feedback or bug reports you send.
  • Ask the Ref: the questions you type into the rules assistant, and its answers.
  • Travel: flights, drives, open carpool seats, lodging and roommate plans you add for tournaments.
  • Expenses: amounts you enter for shared tournament costs, so teammates can split them and settle up by Venmo.
  • Guests: details you enter about people you invite to a tournament, such as their name, email, phone number and dietary needs.

Information collected automatically

  • Identifiers: your account ID, and your device's notification token if you allow notifications. In the current app, each phone is registered separately and removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Read receipts: when you last read each chat conversation, which other people in that conversation can see, and whether you've seen a team announcement in the app, in a notification or by email.
  • Alert delivery: for each push notification, which of your devices it was sent to, whether Apple or Expo accepted it (and why not, if it failed), whether you had that kind of alert turned off or the conversation muted, and when you opened it or read it in the app. Only team admins can see this, never the words of chats they aren't in. It's deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you delete your account.
  • Email open tracking: team announcement emails include a small image that tells us whether the email was opened.
  • Location: your precise location, only while you have location sharing turned on for a tournament map. Teammates can see where you are while it's on. Each location update expires after 24 hours, and your shared location is deleted when you turn sharing off. Location isn't collected at any other time.
  • Activity log: the original app records which screens you open and key actions, such as signing in, so team admins can troubleshoot problems and see how the app is used.
  • Error reports: if the original app crashes, it sends us a description of the error.

Camera, microphone and photos

The apps use your camera only when you choose to take a photo or video to post, and your microphone only to record sound with those videos. Photos and videos you pick from your photo library are uploaded only when you choose to share them. If you save a player card or team photo, the app asks before adding it to your photo library. You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

How we use information

Only to run the team apps: showing rosters, schedules and stats; organizing games, travel, expenses and events; sending team announcements, reminders and messages; answering rules questions; keeping the team space safe; and keeping the apps working. We do not sell information, show ads, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Teammates: other signed-in members can see your profile, the contact details you add to it, your posts, photos, team chat messages and the travel plans you share with the team. Teammates see your birthday as a month and day only. Direct messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.
  • Captains and admins: can also see the information needed to manage the team, such as rosters, RSVPs, guest lists, travel plans, reports and the activity log. Your emergency contact, dietary and allergy info, uniform size, drinking preference and availability are visible only to you, captains and admins.

Ask the Ref and other AI features

When you ask the "Ask the Ref" rules assistant a question, the text of your question and the tournament's rules are sent to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the answer. The app asks for your permission before you send your first question. Google Gemini creates birthday artwork and, in the original app, onboarding portraits and nickname stories, using your profile photo and the profile details involved.

Services we rely on

These companies process information on our behalf, only to provide the features listed:

  • Supabase: hosts the apps' database, sign-in and photo and video storage.
  • Anthropic: answers the questions you type into "Ask the Ref".
  • GIPHY: receives the words you search for when picking a GIF.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you choose it; the Apple Push Notification service, which delivers alerts to the current app; and Apple's music search, which receives the words you search for when you pick entrance or karaoke music.
  • Google: Google sign-in, if you choose it; Google Places, which looks up and shows details and photos for parks, hotels and venues; and Google Calendar, only if you connect it to add team events to your calendar.
  • Weather service (wttr.in): receives the location of a game's park or meet-up spot, never yours, to show the forecast.
  • Resend: sends team emails, such as invitations and announcements.
  • Google Gemini: creates the AI-generated artwork and stories described above.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications for the original app.
  • AviationStack: looks up flight details from the flight numbers you add in the original app.

Links to Spotify, Splitwise, Venmo, YouTube and maps open those services directly, and their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while you're part of the team so the team's history, stats and photos stay intact. When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in and your personal details right away. Game stats and lineups stay, shown as "Former member". Your posts, comments, photos and messages are deleted too if you choose that option; otherwise they stay, shown as "Former member" instead of your name and photo.

Deleting your account

In the current app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. You can also choose Also delete my posts, comments, photos and messages to remove everything you've posted or uploaded. If your account is still pending, the waiting screen has a Delete account button too. You can also email admin@gymclassqueeroes.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you, including your posts if you ask.

Your choices and requests

  • You can view and edit your profile at any time in the app, and leave optional fields blank.
  • You can turn off notifications, location sharing, and camera, microphone and photo access in your iPhone's Settings app or in the app.
  • You can block someone so you no longer see their posts, comments, messages or photos.
  • To get a copy of your information or correct it, email admin@gymclassqueeroes.com from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and the database limits each member to what they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put anything in the app you wouldn't want your teammates to see.

Age

The apps are for adult members of the team and aren't intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and announce significant changes in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: admin@gymclassqueeroes.com.
