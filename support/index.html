Support

Help with the Gym Class Queeroes team app

Email admin@gymclassqueeroes.com and include the email address you use in the app. We usually reply within a few days.

Joining the team

The app is invite-only. A captain or team admin sends you an invitation with an 8-character invite code. On the sign-in screen, choose to join the team and enter your email and that code. If your code has expired or doesn't work, ask a captain to resend it.

Signing in

  • Use the same sign-in method you joined with: Apple, Google, or email.
  • Forgot your password? Choose to sign in with email, then "Forgot password", and open the reset link on the same phone.
  • Signing in with a different method than you joined with can create a second, empty account. If you see an empty profile, email us and we'll fix it.

Reporting a problem or suggesting a feature

In the app, open your profile, then Help & feedback, to send a bug report or idea with screenshots and track its status. You can also email us.

Reporting or blocking someone

  • To report a post, tap its ••• menu and choose Report post. To report a comment or chat message, press and hold it and choose Report.
  • To report or block a member, open their profile and tap the ••• menu.
  • Blocked people can't message you, and you won't see their posts, comments, messages or photos. Unblock anyone in Me → Settings → Blocked people.

Captains and admins act on reports within 24 hours. See the terms for the rules.

Deleting your account

  • In the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm.
  • Turn on "Also delete my posts, comments, photos and messages" to remove everything you've posted or uploaded. Leave it off to keep them for the team's history, shown as "Former member".
  • If your account is still pending, or you haven't finished signing up, you can delete it from that screen too.
  • If you signed in with Apple, you'll confirm with Apple so Apple sign-in is turned off for the app as well.
  • Prefer email? Write to us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Your data

To get a copy of your information, email us from the address on your account. See the privacy policy for what we collect, what's kept and what's removed.

Notifications and location

You can turn notifications, location sharing, and camera, microphone and photo access on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings app, under the Gym Class Queeroes app.
