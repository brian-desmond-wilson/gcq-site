Who can use the app

The app is for team members, captains, substitutes and invited guests who are 18 or older. You need an invitation to join. Keep your sign-in to yourself, and don't share invite codes with people the team hasn't invited.

Be a good teammate

The app is a shared team space. When you post, message or upload:

  • Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate, threats or bullying.
  • Don't share anything that is illegal, sexually explicit, or that you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't share someone else's private information, including outing anyone or revealing details they haven't chosen to share.
  • Only post photos of people who are comfortable being shared with the team, and remove them if asked.
  • Don't try to access information or features you're not meant to have, or disrupt the app.

Captains and admins can hide or remove content and suspend or remove accounts that break these rules. To report content or a member, use Help & feedback in the app or email bwil0007@gmail.com.
