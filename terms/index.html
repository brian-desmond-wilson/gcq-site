Last updated October 27, 2026

These terms apply to the Gym Class Queeroes iPhone apps (our "apps"), run by the Gym Class Queeroes kickball team ("we", "us"). By using the apps, you agree to them.

Who can use the apps

The apps are only for team members, captains, substitutes and invited guests who are 18 or older. You need an invitation to join. Keep your sign-in to yourself, and don't share invite codes with people the team hasn't invited.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

The apps are a shared team space, and we have zero tolerance for objectionable or abusive content and for members who abuse others. When you post, message or upload:

  • Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate, threats or bullying.
  • Don't share anything that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent or otherwise objectionable, or that you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't share someone else's private information, including outing anyone or revealing details they haven't chosen to share.
  • Only post photos of people who are comfortable being shared with the team, and remove them if asked.
  • Don't try to access information or features you're not meant to have, or disrupt the apps.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report content: tap the ••• menu on a post and choose Report post, or press and hold a comment or chat message and choose Report. You can also report an Ask the Ref answer.
  • Report or block a member: open their profile and use the ••• menu to report or block them. You can also block someone when you report something they posted.
  • What blocking does: you no longer see their posts, comments, messages or photos, and they can't message you. They aren't told. Manage blocked people in Me → Settings → Blocked people.
  • You can also email bwil0007@gmail.com to report anything.

Captains and admins act on every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed, and members who posted it or abused others are removed from the apps.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You give the team permission to store and show it to team members inside the apps, and to use it for team purposes such as photo albums, stats and announcements.

Features that use AI

Some features, such as the "Ask the Ref" rules assistant and generated artwork, are created by AI. AI answers can be wrong or incomplete, so don't rely on them alone. Official tournament rules and decisions by referees and organizers always win.

The apps don't process payments. Expense tracking only records amounts; settling up happens in Venmo or Splitwise. Links to Venmo, Splitwise, Spotify, YouTube, maps and hotel booking sites take you to those services, and their own terms apply.

Leaving the team

You can stop using the apps at any time and delete your account in the app under Me → Settings → Delete account, or by emailing us; see the privacy policy. We may remove access when someone leaves the team or breaks these terms.
